Bitcoin Meetup in Parramatta

There is a Bitcoin social night not far from Parramatta, at the Bella Vista Hotel. It is a good way to meet other Bitcoin users without going into the city.

Bitcoin in Parramatta

Bitcoin Sydney North West is a Bitcoin-only social night run by volunteers. People come to learn, ask questions and meet others who use Bitcoin. It is free to attend, beginners are welcome and there is no need to own any Bitcoin.

Getting there by car

From Parramatta, Bella Vista is roughly a 25 to 30 minute drive outside peak times, depending on whether you take the M2 or Windsor Road. Traffic can add a lot, so allow extra time.

Metro and public transport

There is no direct Metro between Parramatta and Bella Vista yet, so most people drive or use the Transport for NSW trip planner to combine bus and Metro. Check the plan for your return trip too.

The venue

Bella Vista Hotel, 13-15 Lexington Drive, Bella Vista NSW 2153.

Why come from Parramatta

Meeting people in person is the fastest way to learn Bitcoin and to find others nearby who use it. Come once, see how you find it, and come back if you like it.
