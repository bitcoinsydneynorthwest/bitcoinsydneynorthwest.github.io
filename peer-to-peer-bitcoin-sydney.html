Peer to peer

Buy Bitcoin with Cash in Sydney: Peer to Peer and Non-KYC

Bitcoin was designed so that two people can trade directly. In Sydney, there is a small and friendly community that still does it the original way: face to face, with cash, and with respect for each other's privacy.

Why peer to peer matters

Peer to peer means you deal directly with another person instead of through a company. There is no middle layer that can freeze your account, change the fees or collect your personal details.

It also builds the local network. When people trade face to face, they meet others who use Bitcoin, which makes it easier to spend, earn and learn.

Keeping cash alive

Cash is private, instant and works without power or internet. Bitcoiners often see it as a friend, not a rival. Cash in, Bitcoin out is a simple swap between two people who both value having an option.

Keeping cash in use matters because the more it fades, the more every payment becomes a record someone else can read.

Non-KYC Bitcoin, explained positively

KYC means 'know your customer', the identity checks that exchanges run. Many people are comfortable with that. Others prefer to keep their financial life private, the same way they would not hand their passport to a shopkeeper.

Choosing non-KYC Bitcoin is about privacy, not secrecy. Everyone should follow the tax and legal rules that apply to them in Australia, and that includes keeping records.

A circular economy

The best part of peer to peer is spending. When you can earn Bitcoin from a local job, spend it at a local business and have that business spend it again, value circulates inside the community.

Our meetup is a good place to hear which Sydney businesses already accept Bitcoin.

Meeting up safely

Meet in a public, well-lit place, ideally at a meetup where others know you. Start small with someone new. Never rush, never send first to a stranger, and check the amount and address twice before confirming. If something feels off, walk away.

We do not run trades or hold anyone's money. The meetup is simply a friendly place to meet people.
