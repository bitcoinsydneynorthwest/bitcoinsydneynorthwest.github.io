Fiat Food and the Case for Sound Money

Fiat Food is a book by Matthew Lishiak that connects how our money works to how we eat. It is a popular read in Bitcoin circles, and a good starting point if you are curious why Bitcoiners talk so much about food.

What is Fiat Food about?

The book argues that money shapes the food system. When money can be created without limit, it rewards short-term thinking, cheap and heavily processed products, and a race to cut costs. It invites readers to ask what a food system built on sound money might look like.

This page is a short introduction, not a summary. Read the book for the full argument and decide for yourself.

Low time preference at the dinner table

Low time preference is the habit of thinking long term. At home, it can mean cooking more, buying better ingredients and supporting local producers. Many Bitcoiners see these as the same instinct that leads them to save in Bitcoin.

Why it connects to Bitcoin

If you believe that sound money encourages people to plan ahead, food is an easy place to see the effect. It is also a gentle way to start a conversation with friends who are not interested in money talk.
